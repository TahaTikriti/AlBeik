We created "Al Beik" to be a place where you can rediscover the joy of Lebanese food, whether you are a fellow expat, or simply a lover of great cuisine. Every meal is crafted with the same passion, care and dedication you'd find in a Lebanese Kitchen. Come and experience the flavors of Lebanon-a little closer to home.
